# Privacy Policy — Waheguru Simran

**Effective date:** September 13, 2026  
**Publisher:** Open Sikh Apps  
**App:** Waheguru Simran  
**App ID (Android & iOS):** `com.opensikhapps.wahegurusimranexpo`

## Summary

Waheguru Simran is a simple offline meditation (simran) player. It plays a single bundled Waheguru Simran audio loop and shows a bundled cover image. The app is designed to work without an internet connection.

## Data we collect

**We do not collect personal data.**

This app:

- Does **not** create accounts or require login
- Does **not** sync data to a cloud service
- Does **not** include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
- Does **not** access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location
- Does **not** send audio, usage history, or identifiers to Open Sikh Apps

## What the app stores and uses on your device

- A bundled audio file and cover image that ship inside the app package
- Temporary playback state while you use play / pause (kept in memory / OS media session while playing)

Nothing else is persisted by the app for profiling or marketing.

## Background audio and lock-screen controls

When you play Simran, the app may continue playback while the screen is locked or the app is in the background. The operating system may show standard media controls (play / pause) and the track artwork on the lock screen or notification shade.

Those controls are provided by the device OS media session APIs. Open Sikh Apps does not receive data from those system surfaces.

## Internet access

The app does not require network access for its core features. If the operating system or app store client contacts the network for updates or store services, that is outside this app’s own behavior.

## Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app’s privacy practices change, this document will be updated. Store listings should link to the hosted copy of this policy when a public URL is required.
